Privacy Policy

How we collect, use, and protect your information.

Effective Date: September 1, 2026  |  Last Updated: September 1, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how AdMediaPixeel LLC ("we," "us," or "our") collects, uses, discloses, and protects your personal information when you use Deepsearch: AI People Finder ("Deepsearch") — our reverse image search mobile application — and related services (collectively, the "Services"). By accessing or using our Services, you acknowledge that you have read and understood this Privacy Policy.

AdMediaPixeel LLC is committed to protecting your privacy and handling your data in a transparent and responsible manner. This policy applies to all users of Deepsearch regardless of location.

1. About Deepsearch: AI People Finder

Deepsearch is a mobile application that allows users to perform reverse image searches on the publicly indexed web. Users select a photo from their device's photo library; that photo is securely transmitted to our backend service, which uses the Google Cloud Vision API (Web Detection feature) to find matching or similar images and webpages across the publicly indexed web. Results are displayed within the app as links to external, publicly available web pages.

Important: Despite its name, Deepsearch does not determine, confirm, or reveal anyone's identity. It searches only publicly indexed web content and shows you links to existing webpages where an uploaded photo already appears. It is not a facial recognition tool and does not itself identify individuals — any identification is something a user may infer for themselves by following the external links in the results, not a determination made by the app. Your uploaded photo is used solely to execute your search request and is not stored permanently on our servers.

2. Information We Collect

2.1 Account Information (Optional)

If you choose to create an account, we collect:

  • Name — provided when you sign in with Google or Apple, used to personalize your in-app experience.
  • Email address — used to authenticate your account, allow password reset, and manage your subscription.
  • User ID — a unique identifier assigned by Firebase Authentication to manage your account and search quota.

Account creation is not required to use Deepsearch. Guest users may use the app and access limited features without providing any personal information.

2.2 Photos You Upload

When you initiate a reverse image search, the photo you select is transmitted from your device to our secure backend server for processing. Uploading a photo is always a voluntary, user-initiated action.

  • Your photo is used solely to perform the search query you requested.
  • To perform the search, your photo is sent to the Google Cloud Vision API (Web Detection feature), which identifies visually similar images and webpages across the public web. Google processes this image on our behalf as our sub-processor and in accordance with its own data processing terms. View Google Cloud Privacy Notice →
  • Photos are not stored permanently on our servers. They are held only for the duration necessary to process and return your search results.
  • We do not perform facial recognition, extract biometric identifiers, build facial profiles, or analyze the identity of any individual in uploaded photos.
  • Photos are never used for advertising, model training, user profiling, or any purpose other than completing your specific search request.

2.3 Subscription and Purchase Data

Deepsearch uses RevenueCat to manage in-app subscriptions. RevenueCat collects and processes:

  • Your subscription status and purchase history.
  • Your User ID (linked to your Firebase account) to associate the subscription with your account.
  • Device-level identifiers used by RevenueCat for subscription validation and fraud prevention.

Payment card information is processed entirely by Apple's App Store. We and RevenueCat never have access to your full payment card number or banking details.

2.4 Automatically Collected Information

Our backend server may log minimal technical data required to operate the service securely, including:

  • IP address and request timestamps (for security, rate-limiting, and abuse prevention).
  • Device type and operating system version (for compatibility and debugging purposes).

We also use Firebase Analytics (a Google LLC service) to understand how users interact with Deepsearch. Firebase Analytics may collect information such as:

  • App usage events (e.g., screens viewed, features used, search actions taken).
  • Session duration and frequency of app opens.
  • General device and app information (device model, operating system, app version, language settings, and an anonymous, non-persistent installation identifier).
  • Approximate location derived from IP address (e.g., country or city level), not precise GPS location.

Firebase Analytics data is used only to help us understand feature usage and improve the app — it is not used to identify you personally, and we do not use it for advertising or sell it to third parties. We do not use any other third-party analytics SDKs (such as Mixpanel) at this time. View Firebase Privacy Policy →

2.5 Search History

Deepsearch stores your search history locally on your device only using your device's local storage. This data is never transmitted to our servers and is not accessible by us.

3. How We Use Your Information

  • Service Delivery: To process your reverse image search requests and return results.
  • Account Management: To authenticate you, manage your account, and enforce search quotas based on your subscription tier.
  • Subscription Management: To verify your subscription status and unlock Pro features via RevenueCat.
  • Security & Fraud Prevention: To detect and prevent abuse, enforce rate limits, and protect the integrity of the service.
  • Legal Compliance: To comply with applicable legal obligations and enforce our Terms of Service.
  • App Analytics & Improvement: To understand how users interact with Deepsearch (via Firebase Analytics) so we can identify popular features, fix issues, and improve the app experience.

We do not use your information for advertising, do not sell it to third parties, and do not use your uploaded photos for analytics of any kind. Our use of app-interaction analytics (described in Section 2.4) is limited to understanding and improving the product.

4. Third-Party Services

Deepsearch integrates the following third-party services:

  • Firebase Authentication (Google LLC): Used for secure account creation, sign-in (including Google and Apple sign-in), and session management. Firebase may collect your email, name, and a unique User ID. View Firebase Privacy Policy →
  • RevenueCat, Inc.: Used to manage in-app subscriptions, validate purchases, and restore entitlements. RevenueCat collects purchase history, subscription status, User ID, and device identifiers. View RevenueCat Privacy Policy →
  • Apple App Store / Sign in with Apple: Used for in-app purchases, subscription billing, and Apple-based sign-in. Apple's privacy practices are governed by Apple's Privacy Policy →
  • Google Sign-In (Google LLC): An optional sign-in method. When used, Google may share your name and email with us for account creation. View Google Privacy Policy →
  • Google Cloud Vision API (Google LLC): Powers the core reverse image search feature. Your uploaded photo is sent to this API's Web Detection feature to find visually similar images and matching web pages. Google processes this data on our behalf and does not retain it beyond what is needed to fulfill the request. View Google Cloud Privacy Notice →
  • Firebase Analytics (Google LLC): Used to understand app usage and improve Deepsearch, as described in Section 2.4. View Firebase Privacy Policy →

5. Data Sharing and Disclosure

We do not sell your personal information. We may share your information only in the following limited circumstances:

  • Service Providers: With RevenueCat and Firebase, solely to operate the subscription and authentication features of Deepsearch.
  • Legal Requirements: When required by law, regulation, court order, or governmental request.
  • Protection of Rights: To protect the rights, property, or safety of AdMediaPixeel LLC, our users, or the public.
  • Business Transfers: In connection with a merger, acquisition, or sale of company assets, with appropriate notice to users.

6. Data Retention

  • Uploaded photos: Not stored permanently. Deleted from our backend servers immediately after the search result is returned to your device.
  • Account data (name, email, User ID): Retained for as long as your account exists. Deleted upon account deletion request.
  • Purchase and subscription data: Retained by RevenueCat in accordance with their retention policy and applicable law.
  • Server logs (IP, timestamps): Retained for up to 90 days for security and abuse prevention purposes, then automatically deleted.
  • Firebase Analytics data: Retained in accordance with Google's default Firebase Analytics retention settings, in aggregated or pseudonymous form for app-improvement purposes.
  • Search history: Stored locally on your device only. Deleted when you clear app data or uninstall the app.

7. Data Security

We implement reasonable administrative, technical, and physical security measures to protect the information we process, including:

  • All data transmitted between your device and our servers is encrypted using HTTPS/TLS.
  • Firebase Authentication enforces industry-standard token-based security for all accounts.
  • Access to backend infrastructure is restricted and monitored.

However, no method of transmission over the Internet or electronic storage is completely secure. If you become aware of any security incident, please contact us at support@admediapixel.com.

8. Your Rights and Choices

Depending on your location and applicable laws, you may have the right to:

  • Access, correct, or delete your personal information.
  • Request a copy of data we hold about you.
  • Withdraw consent or object to certain processing.
  • Delete your account and associated data.

To exercise any of these rights, please contact us at support@admediapixel.com. We will respond within a reasonable timeframe and in accordance with applicable law.

You may also delete your search history at any time directly within the app via the History screen.

9. Children's Privacy

Deepsearch is not directed to children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If we become aware that we have inadvertently collected information from a child under 13, we will take steps to delete it promptly. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us at support@admediapixel.com.

10. International Data Transfers

Your information may be processed in countries other than the one in which you reside, including the United States, where Firebase and RevenueCat infrastructure is primarily located. We take appropriate steps to ensure your information is protected in accordance with this Privacy Policy when transferred internationally.

11. California Privacy Rights (CCPA)

If you are a California resident, you have rights under the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA), including the right to know what personal information we collect, request deletion of your personal information, opt out of the sale of personal information (we do not sell personal information), and non-discrimination for exercising your CCPA rights.

To submit a CCPA request, contact us at support@admediapixel.com.

12. European Economic Area (GDPR)

If you are located in the European Economic Area (EEA), United Kingdom, or Switzerland, we process your personal data in accordance with the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR). The legal bases for processing include:

  • Contractual necessity — to provide the reverse image search service you requested.
  • Legitimate interests — for security, fraud prevention, and service improvement.
  • Consent — for optional features such as account creation.

You may contact us to exercise your GDPR rights, including the right to lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority.

13. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices, technologies, or legal requirements. When we make material changes, we will update the "Last Updated" date at the top of this policy. We encourage you to review this Privacy Policy periodically.
